Final Straw Effect

Created on 2014-07-16 01:53

Published on 2014-07-16 01:53

I think this is a term coined by me, and I have used it very often over the last few years. Felt it would be a nice experience to put it down as a blog.

 

There is this story of a bunch of people wanting to measure the strength and endurance of a camel and they start loading it with straw/hay stacks. The camel stands still until at one moment it just collapses. Was that the final straw which caused the collapse or was it the collective load on its back. Of course we all know it is the collective load, however how often do we see it in our lives.

 

Change is something which we all resist. I remember the days when a new slipper or shoe used to cause skin peel-offs and pain. Then there was the pain of changing a school, changing a teacher, changing a city, language and the list goes on. When this is true about us, we never cease to expect others to change. We refuse to accept that change is a process and it does not happen overnight. I was speaking to a first time manager about his rather thick skinned team member. This person had the unique gift of listening attentively to any feedback and would make one feel that he was going to work on it, however within no time he was back to his old ways. So this first time manager said “I will tell him once, twice, thrice, and if he does not mend his ways, I would just ignore him”. I found myself saying “Project Managers are like parents, we cannot stop trying, we keep trying and keep giving the same message in the process adding to the stack on the camel back. Someday we might place the final straw, or maybe someone else will get the privilege of doing it, yet we would have tried our best to create that impact, we would have added our contribution to the hay stack”.

 

Few years back, I was during a flight I started speaking to a fellow passenger and we started discussing passionately about the world, god and his messages. I shared what I had read newly around that time “God keeps giving us messages/lessons in different forms until we receive it. Once we have received it, he moves on to the next message/lesson”. Interestingly we did not exchange cards or phone numbers. We had passed the messages that we were carrying for one another, thus adding to our own hay stack.

 

Has it happened to you that someone (may be your mother or sister or even a friend) kept telling you some trait in you that they do not like, and you wouldn’t change it until someday, someone would make the same statement and then you would see yourself changing in no time. I call this the Final Straw Effect

 

So if you want someone to change, including yourself, keep giving the message and some day you will see the “Final Straw Effect”

 

A few years back I had the privilege of being interviewed by 3 fresh campus recruits, and found myself telling them motivational stories on how to face failure and life, and later one of them wrote to me stating how what I said changed his life and how he was a better person after that discussion. I thought about it and said to myself “Poor parents and teachers, over the years they would have given the same message hoping to see a change, and here I was, who had the privilege of speaking to this individual for one hour and had the honor of laying the final straw”
