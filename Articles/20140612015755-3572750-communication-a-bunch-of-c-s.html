Communication – A bunch of “C” s

Created on 2014-06-12 01:57

Published on 2014-06-12 01:57

 

My job is fulfilling because it gives me an opportunity to interact with a lot of (young) people who come to me with questions. One of the questions is, I am being asked to improve communication what can I do? What does my manager mean by communication? What they leave unsaid is “I can read and write in English and I have been speaking English for years and nobody seems to have a problem, except my manager during appraisal times”. While I attempted to answer as to what is communication, the thought started developing that Communication is a bunch of “C”s

I generally found myself starting with three main C’s of Comprehension, Confidence and Clarity. Then while trying to explain these three, I end up with more C’s to support the thought. Comprehension – Is the ability of an individual to understand something which another individual is trying to put across or communicate and understand it the way the other person intended it to be understood. Well you might say you just wrote a complex sentence for an easy activity. We all listen to others and understand what they are saying. The problem lies in “the way the other person intended it to be understood”. It so often happens that a manager explains a task to an individual and the individual goes back thinking he has understood. Only upon the completion of the task, does the manager realize that he was not understood. Then the manager thinks that the individual has Communication (Comprehension) problems So what can one do? A solution to that is Confirm. One of the simple techniques to ensure that we have understood what the speaker intended us to understand is to rephrase it in simple words. Many a times “Let me rephrase what you said in my words” and then putting it in simple points, helps get the confirmation from the speaker that we have understood. He might correct us stating, “Well you got point 1 and 3 right, but I meant something else when I said point 2”. This iterative dialogue helps ensure that we have understood something the way speaker intended us to understand it. We have covered one part of communication which is all about Comprehending and Confirming. Let’s move on now Confidence– Many a times when someone speaks about a topic, we get impressed with the confidence with which they are communicating. There are people who can speak confidently about topics which they don’t know anything about, let us put them aside. Then there are people who speak confidently because they have Content. So we can deduce that to speak confidently one needs to have Content. So how does one build content? If one were interested in building content in an area, the person starts reading about it, listening to others who have a point of view on the topic and finally start writing about it. So Content comes with Concentrated effort. When we focus on a topic we are said to be concentrating on the topic and I can deduce that Concentration is key to developing Content We have now covered another part of communication which is about Confidence which comes with Content and which in turn is the result of Concentration. Let’s move on now Clarity – I have marveled at the ability of some individuals who can put cross their thoughts and feelings with Clarity. So what is Clarity? The ability to communicate to others and ensure that they have understood what is being communicated the way you intended them to understand. Now there are times when we need to get work done through others. Delegation becomes an important part of growth of a professional and it is important to be able to communicate with Clarity. So how do we ensure that the other person has understood? Well, this is how I would do it “now that I have communicated what I want to be done, can you please put across what you have understood?” This makes the other person put across his/her understanding in words and it gives me an opportunity to ensure that I have been understood. If I am not convinced with the way another person has put across my requirements/thoughts, I can correct the person and ensure that the communication cycle is complete. So by now you would have started feeling that Comprehension and Clarity are Complimentary as it means the same but from different points of view of the speaker and listener. So to conclude, if the people you are talking to are able to appreciate your Comprehension, Confidence, Content, Concentration and Clarity they think you are Capable and that leads them to think you are Competent and Commend (appreciate) you. On the contrary if they doubt your Comprehension or Content or unhappy about lack of Clarity in your communication, then they start doubting your Capability and Competence. Then this starts appearing in your feedback that you need to improve your communication. Please note that communication is not about accent or vocabulary as many of us would have thought. I have worked with some spirited individuals who have a regional accent to their English or poor English vocabulary as their schooling had happened in regional languages and not English. They never ever let this come in their way of growth as there had good Comprehension abilities and were able to speak with Confidence and Clarity. On the contrary I have interacted with convent educated people who have good accent and vocabulary, however have not been very successful. So here is communication decoded for you as I have understood it :)
Next time your manager tells you to improve your communication, look at what they mean through these new lenses that we have discussed. P.S. – Please note that I am not an authority on communication and have got a comment in my appraisal this year that I need to improve on my communication :)
