In my previous blog I had shared my opinion on why knowing other's perceptions about us is important and had also promised to write about how to proactively manage perceptions.

I had attended a workshop named “My Persona” and the matrix below is in a way a reflection of what I had learnt (It has been 2 years, so this might not be the most accurate interpretation)

Two years back I had written a blog named “What do you want to be known as” highlighting the importance of Self Branding and Self Marketing. I believe it is important for us to first know what do we want to be known as, and then work in the direction of making it happen. I believe we need to consider ourselves as a brand and work on brand building.

Few months back a colleague called me to let me know that he is on his way out and wanted some advice from me for his future. I had given him two pieces of advice, the first one is SSA-Strength, Stakeholder, Action – Framework and the second one is the importance of networking.

I will elaborate the first one here as I believe it is very relevant in the context of this blog.

We all have some strengths that we know of and tools like Johari window allow us to know more. Exercises like SWOT analysis when applied at personal level will help us identify our strengths.

As a next step we should identify the Stakeholders that we would want to impact and then finally come up with actions we would take to make the impact.

As you can see even the public blogging I do in LinkedIn is a planned exercise, I am just using my strength of being able to write. 

In conclusion, Should I worry about other's perception about me?, answer is definitely yes. Why? Perception Matters, Perception lasts and Perception travels, so better take perceptions seriously. How do you go about? Create a conducive environment for feedback so that you receive candid feedback which is a mirror to the perceptions others hold of you. Have a planned approach to your career and life and execute it like you would execute any project.

Most importantly, work on building perceptions about you proactively, so that even perceptions about you are controlled by you (to the extent possible)

Like any plan, this plan would be meaningful only if conducted in the true spirit of professionalism. Else it will turn out to be Boss management and might lead to you building newer facades (as in Johari Window). The objective of this blog is not to promote building façade’s but in the true spirit of self-development work on understanding perceptions that people have about us and promote the right perceptions and fix the negative ones.
