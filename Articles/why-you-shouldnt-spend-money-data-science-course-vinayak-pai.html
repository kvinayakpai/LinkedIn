Why you shouldn't spend money on a Data Science course

Created on 2017-04-08 10:38

Published on 2017-04-08 10:48

In the last few weeks, I have been discussing this topic with many people, let me share two different conversations here

Conversation 1

Aspirant – I want to know what you think about this course, they tell me that I need to register by Monday

Me – Tell them you are not interested (forcing people to sign-up by a deadline is an old sales trick)

Aspirant – Silence

Me – Why do you want to do this course?

Aspirant – The syllabus is good

Me – Why do you want to do this course?

Aspirant – I want to learn Data Science

Me – Why

Aspirant – Everybody is doing it

Me – ha ha ha, That’s precisely why you shouldn’t spend money on this or any other course, it’s a money minting business

Aspirant – Smile

Me – Take the syllabus and find free content on the web for that syllabus and you would find that there is standard content available in Coursera, Udacity and Khan academy. Spend the money that you were planning to spend on some standard industry certifications like Spark and Hadoop certifications from MapR, Hortonworks or Cloudera.

Aspirant – Puzzled

Me – You would be wondering why not any Data Science or R certifications, the next wave of requirements would be on Data Science at Scale, and a Spark certification would help you be there. So definitely learn R from Coursera but also learn SparkR

Me – So to summarize, the skills you need are R, Python, Spark, Hadoop (big data basics) and cloud (basics)

Me – Don’t spend the money on courses but spend on certifications. For learning, you need something more important than money, TIME

Aspirant – Nodding in agreement

Me – Do you know why people join a Gym?

Aspirant – Puzzled

Me – They do it so that they are forced to spend their “time” in the Gym for exercising, which they wouldn’t have spent otherwise. The fact that they have spent their money forces them to spend their “time”. So the real solution is “time” and money kind of forces the discipline

Me – So do a course if you want to force yourself to spend the “time”, if not follow the approach we discussed

Aspirant – Convinced and walks away with a smile (I smile thinking that he might still enroll)


Conversation 2

Aspirant – I want to know what you think about this course, I need to register soon as the last day is approaching

Me – Why do you want to do it

Aspirant – I want to learn data science

Me – Why do you want to spend money on that

Aspirant – Puzzled

Me – You are a senior person (he is a DW Architect with special focus on data integration), you can learn it from online courses easily and use the money elsewhere

Aspirant – ?

Me – Refer my blog on “How to start a data science journey” where I have explained the steps, I think you can directly start with learning Statistics from Khan academy, R/Python from Coursera

Me – More importantly the money that you had put aside for this course, spend it on building your online portfolio of solutions

Aspirant – Puzzled

Me – We work for a services company and for a customer, whatever work we do for the customer is customer’s IP, whatever we do for our company is company’s IP, so what do you have to showcase your expertise

Aspirant – Silence

Me – As you learn from the courses mentioned above, start building the solutions that you have in your mind, online on your own cloud instance

Me – Participate in competitions from Kaggle and AnalyticsVidhya and then add those solutions to your portfolio

Me – So to summarize, the skills you need are R, Python, Spark, Hadoop (big data basics) and cloud (basics), learn these from open domain and then do Spark and Hadoop certifications from MapR, Hortonworks or Cloudera

Me – Build your own portfolio of solutions on cloud and spend money on solving problems and getting certifications which can get you a project in Data Science

Aspirant – Thanks, I have always had a doubt on where to start and got conflicting answers from various people, now I am clear

Me – Yes, the answers should be personalized for individuals, the answer given to an Architect has to be different and suiting him


To summarize, there is huge demand for data science and a whole lot of people want to get into it and are willing to spend money. Then there are also organizations who specialize in training and want to make the best of this opportunity. I had written a blog sometime back “How to choose a data science course? do you become a data scientist with a certification?” where I had even quoted eminent educationalists commenting on this trend. The situation has only worsened and lot of young and old people are ready to spend their hard earned money in the hope that they would be able to get on to the bandwagon and wouldn’t be left behind. 

So if you are a Data Science aspirant, the message for you
  • Look at what the course offers to you
  • Ask yourself why you want to do the course
  • See if the course offers you a job or does is act like a costly Gym membership, and is mechanism to force your “time” in one direction and help you focus on your end objective
  • Invest TIME which is more important than “money”
  • Understand your learning style and if your learning style is Social or if you like classroom based learning, then definitely spend money on the course, If not, learn from open domain
  • Spend money on certifications which have a “job/project fetching” value
  • Spend money on building your online solution portfolio, interviews in the future would be around these rather than paper portfolios
  • Spend money wisely, may be you don’t need that Data Science course that costs a bomb


The market is already getting flooded with profiles of people who have done a data science course from a foreign university or a premier institute and leaving them thinking that they have conquered it all, more about that some other time.
