What do you want to be known as

Created on 2014-06-12 01:54

Published on 2014-06-12 01:54

We have all grown up hearing if we work hard, we will get rewarded in the end. This has given birth to a breed of “Hard Workers” who work so hard that they go un-noticed and hence they don’t get the promised reward.

I believe this is the age of smart working and it is very important to work on Self Branding and Self Marketing. There could be people who are good at taking credit for other people’s work and are good at positioning themselves as the hard workers, even though they hardly work. Let us keep them aside. There could be people who really don’t care for appreciation and who believe in doing their work for the satisfaction it gives them and don’t mind if they are not noticed. Let us keep them aside too. This discussion is about people who work hard, day after day, throughout the year, and feel their effort goes un-noticed. They are craving for recognition and are unhappy because the work they do is not getting them the due recognition. Let us look at this topic differently. If we were to launch a product what are the things we would do

 

  1. We will finalize features of the product
  2. We will brand the product
  3. Identify key stake holders for the success of this product
  4. Come up with a plan to win the stake holder confidence and work on making the product a success
  5. Keep looking at the product performance in the market and bring in newer variants
  6. Continue marketing the changed product

 

Now let us look at the above points from a different point of view. What do we want to be known as? For example, I want to be known as a “Learning Catalyst”

 

  1. Identify the qualities required in a “Learning Catalyst”
  2. Brand myself as “Learning Catalyst”
  3. Identify those key stake holders who should know me as “Learning Catalyst”
  4. Come up with a plan to win stake holder confidence in my ability as “Learning Catalyst”
  5. Identify what are the other features that I would want in my Brand of “Learning Catalyst” and if the features are very different then rebrand
  6. Continue marketing the new Brand As you can see there is an effort involved in making yourself known, known for something, known as someone.

 

Gone are the days when you would do hard work and wait to be recognized. I am not stating that hard work is no more the main ingredient, I am just stating that if you want to be recognized for the work you are doing then it is important to identify the Brand that you want to be known as and market yourself.
