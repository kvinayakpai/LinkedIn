The invisible power behind Data Science

Created on 2016-12-30 03:21

Published on 2016-12-30 03:24

While I was explaining Data Science to a bunch of professionals, I found myself saying this “Data has been there for eternity, ever since humans learnt to capture and store data on the walls of the caves, Science with respect to data has been there for over 50 years now, what makes it relevant today is The invisible power behind Data Science – Business Value”

If you are reading this blog, you either know me or you are curious to know about the hyped term “Data Science”. So why is this term hyped?

What is Data? In layman language any number, text or information which makes sense and is captured for further analysis.

What is Science with respect to Data? In layman language making sense of that data.

John Tukey wrote a paper named “The Future of Data Analysis” in 1962, so that means the Science has existed for over 50 years.

So if Data was always there and if Science with respect to data existed for decades if not centuries, then why is “Data Science” the hyped term?

In today’s materialistic world, every hype has a business opportunity associated with it. Due to the improvements in data storage and data processing technologies, businesses believe that “Business Value” can be derived out of vast amounts of data that they have stored over years, vast amount of “Dark data” that lies idle in the enterprise and humongous amount of data being generated across the globe.

In summary it is this perceived “Business Value” that is “The invisible power behind Data Science”
