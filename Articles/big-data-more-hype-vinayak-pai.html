Is Big Data no more a hype?

Created on 2015-09-13 10:20

Published on 2015-09-13 10:29

Is Big Data no more a hype? Gartner thinks so in the latest hype cycle release http://www.gartner.com/newsroom/id/3114217

 I have been grappling with this phenomenon called “Big Data” for last few years. Few years back, my organization was associated with building the world’s largest citizen identification program and I got exposed to this world of big data during those days.

I was interviewing Architects for the program and found that there were none in the market who really knew about it. Fast forward 5 years and we still do not find people who really know it. They can talk about it, but they do not KNOW it one inch below the surface.

 Some time back a colleague put it rather interestingly, “Big Data is using the principles of Distributed Storage (HDFS) and Distributed Computing (MapReduce) which have been around for ages, so not really sure what the hype is all about”.

 I had written earlier about the book named “Human face of big data” ( http://humanfaceofbigdata.com/ or http://www.amazon.in/The-Human-Face-Big-Data/dp/1454908270), which helped me understand big data better from a practical usage perspective.

 I have been following Gartner Hype Cycle for the last few years and have seen the entry of both “Big Data” and “Internet of Things(IOT)” together in 2011. While “Internet of Things” still remains at the “Peak of Inflated Expectations”, big data has disappeared.

 There are multiple articles around why Big data is not there in Gartner’s hype cycle. It is because big data is no more hype

 Betsy Burton from Gartner talks about hype cycles in General in the video in the link below

http://www.gartner.com/technology/research/hype-cycles/

 She says “But what’s happening is that big data has quickly moved over the Peak of Inflated Expectations,” she continues, “…and has become prevalent in our lives across many hype cycles. So big data has become a part of many hype cycles.”

 The same topic is further discussed by various other sites

 http://www.theregister.co.uk/2015/08/21/forget_big_data_hype_says_gartner_as_it_cans_its_hype_cycle/

 http://www.datasciencecentral.com/profiles/blogs/big-data-falls-off-the-hype-cycle

 Fine, what do I think

 So. what if big data has moved out of the hype cycle, IOT which made appearance along with Big data is still there. There are entries like Machine Learning, Citizen Data Scientist, Advanced Analytics With Self-Service Delivery and Smart Dust. All these contribute to the Data/Information Age we are in.

 Data was there, is there and will be there. A new revolution started with the term big data, however data, big or small is of no use until it is analyzed and acted upon. Over the last few years the focus was on building systems to support the big data, and the focus of the future would be on analyzing this data. As long as there is data, data analysis is there.

 This is how I would look at the emergence of data through the years! 

  • There was a time when number crunching was an area of academics and defense, this slowly moved mainstream over the years
  • There were two types of analysts, people who would do visual analysis (manual) and people who would do programmatic (automated) analysis
  • As the data volumes increased, visual analysis manually became more and more difficult. The visual analysts got sophisticated advance visualization tools to aid them, but still there was the element of manual analysis
  • On the other hand automated analysis led to Machine Learning algorithms which automated the task of visual discovery and analysis. This gave rise to the Data Scientist who would work with these sophisticated techniques
  • Data generation is not going to stop, data volumes would increase and more automation would kick in
  • For the age where data is generated by the “things” (There are more sensors in this world than people) , for meeting the requirements of the “things” (the future is of smart digital assistants who make decisions for humans”, we will need to have “things” (AI system) doing data analysis – This in my opinion is the future of Data Analysis

 I would take few steps back and look at something else. “Digital Transformation” is something which every organization is going through and which everybody is talking about.

As consumers get replaced with things, the amount of digital data generated would increase. With internet of things the data volume would explode. Enter “Smart Dust” and data generation would reach unprecedented levels. (Small data centers, got replaced by big data centers, and in the future would get replaced by micro data centers. Long range wireless, with medium range wireless, and they with small range wireless and eventually Smart Dust with micro range wireless)

In a nut shell, data is here to stay. Small has become big, structured has become multi-structured, volumes have exploded, variety is ever increasing and velocity is showing no signs of slowing down.

 While big data has gone out of the hype cycle, it is there all around us.  Other technological advances mentioned in the Hype cycle report like Bioacoustic Sensing and Brain-Computer Interface in addition to IoT and Smart Dust are going to lead to a scenario like “data, data everywhere, not a drop to think”

 As I sit in my house with the Wi-Fi router connecting a slew of electronic things (gadgets) to each other and to the internet, I wonder if I were able to see data as it moves around me in the air!
