What does one mean when they say you have an attitude?

Created on 2014-06-12 01:56

Published on 2014-06-12 01:56

What does my manager mean when she says “You have got an attitude problem”, I asked her to explain, but she was unable to explain. Can you tell me what does one mean when they say we have got attitude?

This is a snippet from a conversation few years back, I have been trying to answer that question since then and I have been refining my answer. I believe it has reached a stage where I can share it with others. I have arrived at (Please note that this could be a milestone and not the destination) a single line answer for that, Attitude is lack of Humility. Let me now go ahead and elaborate, what is humility. When a person is considered successful, if that person continues to interact with his/her feet firmly on the ground, if the people around them can’t differentiate their behavior in the pre and post states of success and accomplishment, they are titled humble else they have “an attitude” If success is the root cause of attitude, then let us look at a more fundamental question, “How to handle success?” Success is like beginner’s luck in gambling. It brings in the intoxication so intense that we never cease to crave for it. The people who face success early on in life, do not know how to manage it, they start considering themselves as an exquisite specie and start believing that they are superior. This starts superiority complex about themselves when they are with people they consider inferior and inferiority complex when they are with people they consider superior. By the time they learn that what trouble Success has gotten them into there are high chances that they are known for their attitude. So what is success? It could be the sense of financial well-being, it could be the feeling of scoring well in academics, it could be the feeling of getting placed in a dream company or it could be the feeling of getting recognized by their managers/leaders. So how does one manage success? The root lies in communication. Communication happens through verbal and non-verbal means. Success impacts the brain first, the body next and finally the communication. We start oozing the “Superiority Complex” that others start perceiving as “an attitude”. Now to the solution, I had said “Attitude is the lack of humility”, so let us now look at what is humility and how can we attain humility, because that is the antidote for the intoxication that success brings. No matter how successful a person becomes, with the antidote of humility, he/she starts treating everyone else as passengers in the same journey as they are in and starts treating the fellow passengers with humility. Many times people don’t know that they don’t know. Only when we start understanding what we don’t know or the vastness of what we don’t know, we start being humble. If we stand at the altar of knowledge and if we can feel it’s vastness, it is the beginning of knowledge. When we know its vastness we start knowing how little we know and how much more is there to be known then we start being humble. Let us pick up one topic say “Big Data Analytics” as this is the example I have been using of late. There are a bunch of people who are undergoing a training program on Big Data Analytics. · This program would cover areas like open source technologies, Java, Hadoop, Map Reduce and the Big Data farm (as I lovingly call it as it has lots of animals in it), Analytics – specifically R statistical programing language, Database/Data Analysis skills, Business Knowledge etc., · There are different people who have come with some knowledge in some of these areas. · Any training in a topic would give us roughly around 10% knowledge of that area. So at the end of the program people would be having different levels of knowledge, someone who didn’t have any background would have at least 10% of the knowledge and someone who came into this program with some project experience in Big Data could would have close to 50%. Over a period of time when these people start working in these technologies their knowledge in this area might go up and reach around 70%. · Some of them who have excellent communication skills might start teaching it and as they become successful teachers, they might reach around 90%. · If one continues they journey towards expertise in a quest for excellence then he/she would need to learn continuously Let us relook at Journey to expertise Trained in a particular area– 10% Prior knowledge + Training + Lots of effort in knowing more about the area – 50% Working on challenging projects in the area– 70% Teaching all levels of people and ensuring they understand – 90% Continuous Learning – ~95% So when one is continuously learning, one knows how much he/she knows and how much more is there to know, so they are grounded and humble. Summarizing it, Attitude comes with not knowing how to manage success, one of the ways of tacking attitude is by developing humility, humility comes when we know that we don’t know (or how little we know) and humility gets stronger through Continuous Learning Continuous Learning = Continuous Breathing. When we stop breathing we die and when we stop learning we die an intellectual death or in the corporate context we die a Corporate Death
