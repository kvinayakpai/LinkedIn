Quest for Excellence - Summary

Created on 2014-07-26 02:12

Here is how I would like to summarize this series on “Quest for Excellence”

Personal Excellence

  1. Love Change or you will become extinct
  2. Take Risks, ask yourself (What is the worst case scenario? What is the best case scenario? What is the realistic scenario?)
  3. Make Mistakes – learn from them and don’t repeat them
  4. Learn something every day – Continuous Learning is like Continuous breathing
  5. Set Goals - break them into ones that are bite size and manageable
  6. Ask for criticism – your worst critics are your best pals in your growth

Service Excellence

  1. Inspire people to be their best each and every day
  2. Be a bureaucracy buster - capture data about why a process is not right, what are the ways of fixing it, and then try to change it
  3. Keep asking what’s new and what next – Be always alert to the changing world and make people around you also be prepared for change

Leadership Excellence

  1. Leaders lead by example – Be the change that you want to see around you
  2. Listen – Be an active listener and connect to people through points they made during their talk
  3. Create the vision and dreams – If Victor Frankyl could survive the Nazis with his power of vision, we could survive any adverse situation
  4. Provide the energy and enthusiasm – Let people draw from your energy and you draw from them
