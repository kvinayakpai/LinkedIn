This is an email that I send to people I come in contact with. I believe that we should plan for our careers and should constantly work on building our own brand value. I believe that continuous learning helps us to keep ourselves up to date. In that regard, I believe the information below would be useful to you.

 Wish you all the best in your career journey. Happy Learning

 Big Data

===========

Best place to start learning Big Data is http://bigdatauniversity.com/. Start with something like Hadoop fundamentals

http://bigdatauniversity.com/wpcourses/

You could also do a course from Coursera about Introduction to Data Science

https://www.coursera.org/course/datasci

 

There is a book named Human face of big data, read the flip book on their web site

http://thehumanfaceofbigdata.com/

This is big data explained through stories where it touches human mind
