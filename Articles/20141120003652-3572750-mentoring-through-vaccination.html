Mentoring through vaccination

Created on 2014-11-20 00:36

Published on 2014-11-20 00:36

As per Wikipedia

Mentorship is a personal developmental relationship in which a more experienced or more knowledgeable person helps to guide a less experienced or less knowledgeable person.

Vaccination is the administration of antigenic material (a vaccine) to stimulate an individual's immune system to develop adaptive immunity to a pathogen.

I have observed a weakness in myself. Many a times I tend to see only what is relevant to me or what I perceive is relevant to me. It is called Selective Perception. For example., I recently got introduced to a company which makes ATM machines and I hadn’t heard of this company before that. Every ATM that I have visited since, I see the name of that Company etched on the ATM machine, perfectly in the field of my vision. I still can’t understand how I haven’t seen it for the last 16 years that I have used ATM machines.

I understand from my reading on the topic so far that Selective Perception is a common phenomenon and all of us are in some way of the other affected by this.

Why is it that I am able to see something during every visit to the ATM now, which I hadn’t seen for years, while it was there all the time? I believe it is because of the awareness of the importance of it for me now

Now linking this thought process to vaccination, by inducing a vaccine into the system, doctors are stimulating the immune system of an individual to develop immunity against a disease. They are introducing the importance of being prepared

Why do I think Mentoring is equivalent to vaccination? Through mentoring we introduce the mentee to new concepts or paradigms which he/she wouldn’t have been exposed to earlier. We make them think along different lines for short periods of time, thus making them come up with an immune system to a risk that might be coming up in the future.

Here are some of the vaccines I use during mentoring

  • What have you learnt new in the last one month
  • What is your plan for your career
  • Are you investing enough to sharpen your skills?
  • Have you heard of the concept of 10,000 hours required for an expert?
  • Are you a generalist or a specialist? Have you heard of T-Shaped professional? Are you worried about your lack of depth?

 

  • Can you say NO?
  • Can you manage FAILURE?
  • What are your fears?
  • Can you forgive low performance?
  • Can you forgive yourself?
  • How do you accept change?
  • Have you thought of the difference between net-worth and self-worth?

 

  • Have you thought of Work life balance?
  • Do you have a hobby outside of work that you are passionate about?
  • Are you spending enough time on your hobby?
  • Do you think your health is getting affected by work related stress?

 

  • Do you consider yourself successful?
  • Do you think you are obsessed with perfection, should you be?
  • Have you felt success is intoxicating?
  • Have you seen the darker side of success?
  • Do you know what people think about you? Does that matter?
  • Have you heard of Johari Window? What do you think about some façades that you might be wearing? What is the right time to remove them?

 

  • Are you doing structured innovation at work?
  • Is your manager pushing you enough? Have you heard that leaders job is to take people out of their comfort zone?
  • Do you see yourself as a leader?
  • Have you thought about the concepts of Individual leadership, customer leadership and thought leadership?
  • Can you do Context switching comfortably, talk to different people about different things and get to differing levels of detail depending on the audience?
  • How good are you at listening?
  • When was the last time you empathized with someone?
  • When was the last time you motivated someone?
  • When was the last time you inspired someone through your action and not words?
  • Are you a role model to someone?

 

  • What makes you tick? What gives you the high?
  • What is more important Direction or Destination?
  • Can you think like a startup

 

Depending on who I am working with, the questions, the tone, the intensity varies. I believe that through these questions, I am vaccinating them against some of the challenges that they might face in their careers like feeling or getting outdated/obsolete, managing change or failure, work like balance, impact of stress on health, managing success, managing feedback, managing leadership and keeping oneself motivated.

I believe it is possible to vaccinate through mentoring.
