Traditional DW/BI covers the descriptive and Diganostic Analytics. In one of its reports Gartner says that only 30% of organizations are using Predictive Analytics and 3% use Prescriptive Analytics.  

(http://searchbusinessanalytics.techtarget.com/feature/Prescriptive-analytics-takes-analytics-maturity-model-to-a-new-level)

 As per latest Gartner hype cycle (http://www.gartner.com/it/page.jsp?id=2124315) Prescriptive Analytics has another 10 years before it finds broad based adoption.

 So when we speak about Advanced Analytics we are really speaking of Predictive Analytics. So what then is Predictive Analytics?

 

 As you can see most of the DW/BI professionals are already half way through in their journey to Advanced Analytics and just need to cover the final mile.

 Most of the Analytics professionals I have interacted with are of the opinion that while statistics is the heart of predictive analytics, understanding of the business processes and ability to work with data play a major role in their day-to-day job.

 There was a time when we were looking at Predictive Analytics practitioners as Business Analyst, Data Analyst and Statistical Analysts. 

  1. A Business Analyst would understand the business processes and would have strong domain knowledge
  2. A Data Analyst would understand business processes and would have the ability to work with data and prepare it for statistical analysis and
  3. A Statistical Analyst would understand business processes and would be able to come up with a solution/algorithm that best suits the business problem at hand

 Today the lines between these three roles have blurred and an Analyst is supposed to be amalgamation of all these three roles.

 So a person who wants to be able to work on Predictive Analytics needs to have the ability to 

  1. Understand business processes
  2. Understand business data and be able to prepare it for statistical analysis
  3. Understand the statistical algorithms and be able to link it to a business problem and a solution

 A person who has worked with traditional DW/BI and has met the pre-requisites mentioned in the earlier part of the blog, has just point 3 to conquer. If one has the first two, I believe some dedicated time and energy and perseverance is all that a DW/BI professionals needs to get into the space of Advanced Analytics or Predictive Analytics space.
