Do Data Scientists do Racial and Gender Discrimination?

Created on 2016-07-01 16:53

Published on 2016-07-01 16:58

Why does a ML system identify a black person as Gorilla? Why does a ML system think a Chinese person is blinking when she is smiling? Why does a webcam refuse to recognize a black person? Why are risk scores generated by predictive algorithms biased against blacks?  Why does a Google ad for highly paid job does not show up for women? (links at the end)

Indeed all these ML algorithms have demonstrated  racial and gender discrimination and the Data Scientists who wrote these are the root cause. But are they?

How does a Machine Learning Algorithm work? In a language understandable to layman, the person who programs it teaches it, how to work. For example, He shows it pictures of thousands of apples and then teaches it to identify a apple from a photograph. When given a set of new photographs, the program is able to identify apples. So the way an Algorithm works is as good as it has been taught. So if there are biases that a programmer has, it is quite possible that these could be passed on to the program, just like children imbibe certain qualities from their parents or surroundings.

 The above type of ML Algorithm is called supervised learning where the Data Scientist working with it, trains it to do a task.

 The other type of ML Algorithm is called unsupervised learning where the Data Scientist teaches some basics to the program and exposes it to vast amounts of data, letting it learn from the data.

 An example for that was Tay, a Twitter bot created by Microsoft and given the identify of a teenage girl. The bot learnt from the people it interacted with and within 24 hours became a Hitler loving sex robot. Microsoft took it offline immediately.

 Just like children who are exposed to big bad world turn bad, Tay became a bad girl.

 That brings us to the question, if Data Science is the future, how do we ensure that the algorithms that we write do what they are supposed to do and not deviate. We need to be able to teach these programs just like we teach our children “Culture” of what is good and bad, what is right and wrong, how to identify bad influence, how not to be biased , basically we need to do better parenting.

 Or should we create a curriculum on  moral science for these algorithms?

 Alfred Nobel who invented Dynamite, never expected to be called “Merchant of Death”, similarly if the Data Scientists do not want to be labeled as Racial and Gender Discriminators, they would need to be careful.

 It is just the beginning of the Data Science era and the future would unfold this and other such challenges for them.

 In conclusion, a line that I liked from the articles below “machine-learning systems don’t understand the difference between mistaking a chimp for a gorilla, which may be OK, and mislabeling a human as a gorilla, which is offensive.”
