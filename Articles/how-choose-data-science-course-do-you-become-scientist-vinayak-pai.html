I have been speaking about Data Science & Data Scientists at various events and have been blogging on this topic and directing people to my blogs. Over the last few days, I have had this growing doubt, “what if I am wrong”?  As per the quote above, with knowledge doubt increases. While there were discussions around various themes around Data Science, I have picked quotes which are in a way substantiating my views :)

  • When we hire an Analytics professional from the market, and assign him to a project, the question they ask is “when can I get the csv file” – Dr. N.R.Srinivasa Raghavan
  • There is a lot of work which happens before we reach the csv file stage (and they don’t seem to understand) – Kiran
  • How do you tell a story becomes very important and it is a must have skill – Dr. N.R.Srinivasa Raghavan
  • Knowledge of new age, big data skills is also required – Dr. N.R.Srinivasa Raghavan
  • Subject matter expertise (domain knowledge) is very important – Dr. N.R.Srinivasa Raghavan
  • While there are lots of Data Science and Business Analytics courses, quality and depth of these courses is concerning – Prof. Dinesh Kumar
  • Data Science – is a journey, you can’t become a data scientist once you get a certificate – Prof. Dinesh Kumar
  • I have taught Math for 20 years and Analytics for 10 years, still there are things that I don’t know and I continue to learn – Prof. Dinesh Kumar
  • Young people think that Data Science is all about Math Modeling and get frustrated when they find out it isn’t – Dr. N.R.Srinivasa Raghavan
  • Fair amount of time is spent in understanding the business process, data and business problems – Dr. N.R.Srinivasa Raghavan
  • Today there are courses which claim to be Business Analytics/Data Science but they are basically teaching Math/Statistics disguised as Business Analytics – Prof. Dinesh Kumar
  • A course without management thinking, without Industry involvement and with math disguised as Analytics is not worth – Prof. Dinesh Kumar
  • Analytics is not about R and Python – Prof. Dinesh Kumar
  • Problem solving is what is more important – Prof. Dinesh Kumar
  • A certification from a premier institute like IIM which covers all the aspects above is worth the investment – Prof. Dinesh Kumar

 (I could have got the quotes wrong or the names wrong, but believe that I got the spirit behind them)

10-15 years back, professionals believed that a master’s degree in management is what adds value to their career and everyone wanted to do a MBA. Time has shown that it is not about an MBA degree, but from where you get it and how you get it. We know today that an full time MBA from a premier institute is worth much more than a distance education MBA from some institute. The same is true with a Data Science course. Premier institutes spend  a lot of effort in designing a course and then bring in the best faculty to teach and then expose the students to the Industry.

 In summary, it is important to check what you are getting out of a Data Science course rather than doing any course. It should have rounded content, it should have business/industry/domain component and it shouldn’t be purely stat based or tool based certification.

 Would like to reiterate what I think of as the key skills for a Data Science career – updated since I wrote the “6 skills blog” 

  • Good Communication skills
  • Learn how to learn - Know your learning style
  • Data/programming (SQL + Excel + R + Python + Scala + Spark + D3JS + Cloud + Big data skills)
  • Problem solving skills (link problem-algorithm-solution)
  • Presentation/Visualization skills
  • Interpersonal skills

 In conclusion, I would like to use the quote from the session – “Data Science – is a journey, you can’t become a data scientist once you get a certificate”. Happy learning…
