How does a data professional prepare for a recession

Created on 2023-01-05 03:25

Published on 2023-01-05 03:31

Change, is the only constant. Janus, is the god of Change. The month of January is named after Janus.

Many years ago, I heard Krishnan Raman , one of my leaders, say BI is a Janus faced technology. In good times It is used for analyzing and predicting growth and in not so good times, it is used for analyzing spend.

It is interesting that I am thinking of Janus, in the month of January, when the world around me is changing.

For years, I have been asking my teams to focus on their learning and not worry about the impact of the change around them.

As per me, learning is synonymous with growth.

Many years ago, I got introduced to the concept of circle of concern when reading the book 7 Habits of Highly Effective People.

Few years later, I got introduced to circle of control and have been using this as a guide when faced with any concern.

Change is a concern for most of us and how to effectively manage change is a challenge that we need to deal with at regular intervals in our lives.

During my mentoring sessions, I use all of these concepts to prepare people for the change.

I urge them to look at what is in their control and focus on that rather than be concerned about what they can’t control or have little control over.

I strongly feel that “Continuous Learning” is what is under our control. I believe “strong focus” on work that is “in our control” is the way forward.

Very often, I am faced with people who want to “Change” their career, move from what “they are doing” into what “they want to do”.

My advice for them, is to focus on what is under their control, namely the role they are playing currently and come out as the best in it. At the same time, focus on “Learning” the skills required for doing “what they want to do”.

Be the best in what they do, and in parallel prepare for the future.

This is my “Mantra for Change” which seems to have helped me guide a lot of careers.

Now coming to the question of “How does a data professional prepare for a recession”. Answer should be evident by now, “Be the best in what they do”, and in parallel focus on picking up new age skills which help them face the transforming world in front of us.

We are facing Change with a Janus face, focus on what is current and could soon be past, prepare for what is future and soon could become current.

I will conclude with a story that I had heard from Subroto Bagchi during the last recession. During a high tide, the land adjacent to the sea gets flooded and creatures (strong and weak) from the sea come to the land and feast on food from the land. During a low tide, immediately after the high tide, as the water recedes, it leaves many weak sea creatures on the land, vulnerable, to be feasted. The strong creatures on the other hand move with the tide.

Moral of the story, weak creatures are the ones that get impacted during the low tide.

The call that he had given to us at that time, was to be strong.

Coming back to today, as the data professionals are faced with a recession, that is akin to the low tide, I urge them to focus on being strong.

Change is an opportunity as Janus would show us, yin and yang is all about finding opportunities in the face of adversity. So face change, focus on learning, be strong.

Parting words – Focus on learning – 1) Cloud – The face of opportunity, 2) Programming Language like Python, 3) Domain of the data that you are working with and  4)Mathematics that is the backbone of all Analytics.
