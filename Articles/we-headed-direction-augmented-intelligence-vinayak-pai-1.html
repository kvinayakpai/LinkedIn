Are we headed in the direction of Augmented Intelligence?

Created on 2016-08-13 15:08

Published on 2016-08-13 15:16

This blog is an outcome of the reading a bunch of articles (links of which I have provided at the end of the blog).

 We have been reading about digital transformation. . I was at a NASSCOM sponsored event recently where I learnt that MSMB (Micro Small & Medium Businesses in India) are moving in the digital direction and the adoption rate is going to move to 70% in the next three years from the current 12%.

 What this means is that it is no more large corporations that are going digital, almost every Industry is going to be digital in the next few years.

 What does this mean to us? This means there is tons of data getting generated. Over last few years organizations have spent money in building big data solutions which help them bring all internal and external data together. While many of them are battling the problems of Big Data Integration, Data Quality, Data Governance and Master Data Management, the focus is shifting to insights that would come out of this data.

 If I were to compare Data Warehouse of the past to Data Lake of today, then we are going through a phase where Business Intelligence is being extracted out of the Data Lake. Then will come a time when Advanced Analytics would be run on this data and Machine Learning algorithms start churning this data in the hope of nectar of insights that could emerge out of it.

 Real optimization algorithms running on data lake would require quantum computing as the current computing power with all the power of cloud, could still fall short.

 Does that mean AI (Artificial Intelligence) has arrived finally? Does that mean quantum computers are going to take over?

 During my lectures when I talk about Decision Automation, one of the questions I get asked is – “Can machines take decisions? Will this mean humans will not be required? What would happen ……..”

 As Bernard Marr says “Before the invention of the ATM, there were around half a million bank tellers in the US. Today, we have about half a million bank tellers”.  He goes on to add ““So people are pretty good about finding other things to do when key tasks like dispensing cash and so on get taken over by machines. So my argument is that there will be some job losses but they will be marginal rather than dramatic, and the people who are good at working with machines will probably do fairly well at least for a while.”

 Similarly with the advent of Quantum Computing the age of Augmented Intelligence would start. While some mundane repetitive tasks are left for machines, there are some where humans would take inputs from machines and take better decisions. Then there are some Where humans tell machines what problems they need to solve and how.

If this has interested you, then I would recommend you watch this video to understand more about Augmented Intelligence and the power of it 

 

In conclusion, In the Industrial age, when machines came there were protests that machines will take away jobs from humans, yet after few years humans learnt to work with machines, similarly in the IT age, when computers came there were protests that computers will take away jobs from humans, yet after few years the same humans have learnt how to work more productively with the aid of computers, similarly in the data age, when automation kicks in there are conspiracy theories about how automation is going to take away job, yet in few years we will see humans working with automation/robotics, in the world of Augmented Intelligence.
