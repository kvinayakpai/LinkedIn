# Themes in my own writing, by year

What I've written about, counted from my articles and post commentary. It shows what I have said I think, not what I read. The linked articles could not be fetched from the analysis environment, so nothing here is based on them.

## Method

- Source: `Posts/index.json`, 2,366 posts with commentary, plus the 70 articles in `Articles/`.
- Each theme is a keyword pattern matched against the commentary. Counts are posts, not word frequency.
- 58 posts contain AI-generated notes (for example "Notes created by Gemini"). These are included in the counts, but they are not my wording.
- Keyword counts are rough. "AI" also matches inside other words only when they are standalone, but "agent" can mean other things, so treat the numbers as a guide.

## What stands out

- **From data science to AI.** Data science and analytics dominate 2014–2018. AI rises from 2024 and is the top theme in 2025.
- **Agents appear in 2024** and are the main AI theme in 2025.
- **Ethics and trust** rise sharply in 2024–2026. Before 2024 they were rare.
- **Scepticism is a small but steady note,** with a spike in 2026.
- **Learning and careers** are a constant from 2016 onwards.

These are patterns in what I wrote about. They do not describe my personality or my character.
